Phase 1 Exam

Java & Object-Oriented Programming

Prove you are ready for robot code. Twenty-five questions across five sections, mostly reading code rather than reciting definitions.

How this is scored

Each section scores separately and on purpose. One overall number tells you almost nothing. Five section scores tell you which unit to re-read.

  • 4 or 5 out of 5 in a section means you are solid there.
  • 3 out of 5 means re-skim that unit before build season.
  • 2 or fewer means work back through that unit properly. It will cost you an hour now and save you a weekend in February.

Nothing is timed and nothing is recorded. Wrong answers explain themselves, so read the explanation even when you guessed right.
